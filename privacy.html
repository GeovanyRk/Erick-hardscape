Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Monroy Stone Works ("we," "us") collects through this website, stonemonroy.com, and how that information is used.

Information You May Provide

If you choose to contact us through the quote request form on this site, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • The type of project or service you're asking about
  • A message describing your project

We do not collect this information unless you submit it yourself through the form.

How We Use This Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry, answer questions, and provide a quote for the hardscape services you're interested in.

Third-Party Form Processing

Our contact form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. When you submit the form, your information is sent through Formspree's platform so that it can be delivered to us. You can review Formspree's own Privacy Policy for details on how they handle submitted data.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember your language preference (English/Spanish) between visits — this is not used to track you across other websites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at Erik@monroystoneworks.com or (336) 306-3941.
